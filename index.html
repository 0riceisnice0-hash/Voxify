Transparency

Why do I need this?

Normal Voxify effects run entirely inside Chrome. The AI voice is different — here's why a small local helper is needed.

🌐

Normal effects run in Chrome

Standard Voxify pitch, reverb, and voice effects are processed directly inside the Chrome extension with no extra software needed.

🤖

The AI voice uses a larger model

The premium AI Female Voice uses a Python/PyTorch deep-learning model that is far too large and resource-intensive to run inside a browser extension.

💻

The helper runs on your PC

The engine runs locally as a lightweight background process. Your voice is captured, converted on-device, and passed back to Voxify in real time.

🔒

Audio stays on your machine

Audio is processed locally and never sent to Cloudflare or any external server for AI conversion. Your voice data remains on your own hardware.

⏱

Small delay is normal

After you speak, there's a short processing delay while the model converts each phrase. This is expected behaviour — not a bug.

Get started

Install in minutes

Follow these steps to get the AI Female Voice working in Voxify.

1

Unlock Premium on Discord

Join the Voxify Discord server and verify your Premium access to unlock the download.

2

Download Voxify Premium Engine

Grab the Windows installer from the official download link below — never from random Discord attachments.

3

Run the engine

Launch the installer and follow the setup wizard. The engine will start running as a local background helper.

4

Open Voxify → Premium tab

In the Chrome extension, navigate to the Premium tab to access AI voice settings.

5

Click "Check Engine" or "Preload Model"

This confirms the local engine is running and loads the AI model into memory (may take a moment the first time).

6

Start testing AI Female Voice

Enable the AI Female Voice toggle, speak clearly, and experience the conversion. Wear headphones for best results!

Beta notes

Known limitations & tips

Beta
  • This is experimental and still being actively improved — expect changes.
  • Short phrases currently work best for the cleanest conversion.
  • There will be a small delay after you speak while the model processes each phrase.
  • Some voices may sound slightly crackly depending on your mic level and GPU performance.
  • Speaking clearly at a healthy mic volume gives the best result — avoid whispering or shouting.
  • Please send all feedback in the Discord server. It directly shapes what gets improved next.
